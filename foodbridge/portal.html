Food Safety & Hygiene Standards

FoodBridge mandates strict adherence to food safety protocols under public health guidelines before donating or claiming surplus food.

1. Two-Hour Rule: Cooked surplus must be refrigerated below 5°C or maintained hot above 60°C within 2 hours of preparation.
2. Clean Sealed Packaging: Food must be secured in food-grade, sanitized containers or foil lids labeled with dish name & allergens.
3. Freshness Verification: Inspect aroma, visual quality, and safe-to-consume windows before handing to volunteers or distributing.
